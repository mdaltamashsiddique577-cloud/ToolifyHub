Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use ToolifyHub?+

No. There is no login, signup, OTP or email collection. You can use every tool straight away.

Are my files uploaded to your servers?+

No. PDF and image tools process your files in your browser, and the files are not sent to us. The AI summarizer is the only tool that sends text to a server.

Is ToolifyHub free?+

Yes. Every tool on the site is free to use.

Why does the EMI calculator give a slightly different figure from my bank?+

Banks round differently and add their own fees. The calculator uses the standard reducing-balance formula. Use it as an estimate and check the figure with your lender.

Are the GST rates on the calculator current?+

The calculator lets you pick the rate, so you can use the one that applies to your goods or services. Check the official GST portal for current rates, because they change.

Does ToolifyHub store what I type?+

Text stays in your browser. We keep only the names of the six tools you used most recently, in your browser's local storage, and you can clear it at any time.
